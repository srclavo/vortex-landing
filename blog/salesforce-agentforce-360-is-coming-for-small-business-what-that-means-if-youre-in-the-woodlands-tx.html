Salesforce Agentforce 360 Is Coming for Small Business — What That Means If You're in The Woodlands TX

Salesforce Agentforce 360 landed on September 11, and the headline is real: a free AI tier for small businesses. Seven named agents with long-term memory, 200,000 Flex Credits per month, and 1,000 included conversations. The enterprise is officially coming for your market.

Before you cancel your software subscriptions, here's what the free tier actually includes — and where the math breaks down for businesses in The Woodlands and Houston.

What Salesforce Launched

Agentforce 360 introduces seven named agents: Casey (customer service), Paige (IT and HR), Carter (commerce), Hunter (outbound sales — pursues goals over weeks without human intervention), Marshall (supply chain), Piper (inbound pipeline), and Fin (customer experience, acquired September 10). Each agent has memory, a job title, and defined scope.

The SMB free tier bundles in AI email drafting, AI conversation summaries, and 200,000 Flex Credits for businesses on Salesforce's lower-priced tiers. On the surface, that looks like a substantial offer.

What You Actually Get

Here's where it gets specific. One Agentforce conversation costs roughly 100 Flex Credits. At 200,000 credits per month, that's approximately 2,000 conversations — about 65 per day.

For a dental office fielding 30–50 patient inquiries daily, that's workable. Barely. Add scheduling confirmations, review requests, and missed-call follow-ups, and you're over budget before the third week.

For a freight broker handling carrier rate requests, load status updates, and check calls? The free tier runs out before Friday.

There's a deeper issue: Agentforce 360 is built for companies already running on Salesforce CRM. If you're not a Salesforce shop — and most small businesses in The Woodlands aren't — you're not evaluating a product. You're evaluating a $150/user/month ecosystem first.

Why Generic Agents Don't Work for Vertical Businesses

Hunter, Salesforce's outbound sales agent, is impressive. But Hunter doesn't know what a lumper fee is. Casey doesn't know a dental treatment plan from an intake form.

Generic agents are built for generic businesses. A freight broker in Houston isn't a generic business. A dental practice in The Woodlands isn't either.

The ROI in AI automation comes from specificity. RXO, a major freight broker, automated over 500,000 carrier calls in Q1 2026 alone. Quote response dropped from 47 minutes to under 5 minutes. ROI payback landed in 60–120 days. That didn't happen with a customer service agent named Casey — it happened with agents built specifically for freight operations.
