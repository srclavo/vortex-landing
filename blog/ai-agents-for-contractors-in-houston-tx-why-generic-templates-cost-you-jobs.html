What GoHighLevel Field Services Actually Delivers

The GHL Field Services beta ships pre-built workflows: scheduling prompts, job status updates, follow-up messages. For a contractor who has nothing automated today, it's a starting point. It's also one-size-fits-all.

A residential HVAC company in The Woodlands and a commercial plumbing operation in Katy have different quoting flows, different response expectations, different close rates, and different seasonal demand curves. A template doesn't account for any of that. It answers every inbound lead the same way, with the same message, regardless of the job type, the time of day, or the caller's intent.

That's not an agent. That's a canned response with a scheduler attached.

  • What's your follow-up gap? The window between sending an estimate and hearing back is where most contractors lose jobs — and most don't know it.

From those answers, we build agents that reflect the actual business. Lead response under 90 seconds, any time of day or night. Estimate follow-up sequences that run on a cadence your team doesn't have to manage. After-hours qualification so Monday starts with vetted opportunities, not raw callbacks. Review requests triggered after job close.

The agents know your pricing logic. They know when to escalate to a human. They don't ask a commercial client the same intake questions as a residential emergency call.
