What Makes an AI Agent Actually Work

Three things separate an agent that earns its cost from one that gets cancelled:

1. It's tied to a specific outcome. Not "improve customer experience." Something concrete: respond to every missed call within 60 seconds, generate 3 appointment requests per week from Google reviews, handle 100% of after-hours inquiries without voicemail.

2. It runs with business-specific context. A generic AI doesn't know that your dental practice closes at 4 PM on Fridays, that Dr. Martinez handles implants, or that some patients need intake in Spanish. Without that context, the agent gives generic answers to specific questions — and patients notice.

3. Someone owns the number. Every Vortex agent tracks one or two metrics tied directly to revenue: leads responded, appointments booked, reviews answered, follow-ups sent. No dashboard theater — just the number that tells you whether the agent paid for itself this month.
