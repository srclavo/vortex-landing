The Problem: Your Inbox Is Running Your Operation

The average freight broker handles a high volume of repetitive email every week — rate requests, load tenders, carrier availability, check calls, tracking updates. Most of that communication follows the same structure. The same fields, the same back-and-forth, the same process every single time.

When your team handles that manually, each response takes anywhere from 5 to 15 minutes. At 30 to 50 emails a day per operator, you're spending hours on work that doesn't require freight expertise — it just requires fast typing and the right data in front of you.

The margin impact is invisible because it's baked into your overhead. But it accumulates: slower quotes mean missed loads. Delayed responses to carriers mean lost capacity. And your best operators spend their day in their inbox instead of building relationships and closing freight.

What AI Email Automation Actually Does

A properly configured AI agent reads incoming emails, classifies intent — rate request, load tender, carrier onboarding, tracking inquiry — pulls the relevant data from your rate tables and TMS, and drafts or sends the response. Not a generic auto-reply. A real response with the right rates, lanes, and confirmation details for that specific request.

The 80% automation benchmark doesn't mean 80% of your emails vanish. It means 80% are resolved without a human touching a keyboard. The remaining 20% — complex negotiations, exception handling, relationship calls — stay with your team, because those actually require judgment.

That's the real shift: your operators spend their time on the 20% of work that requires a freight professional, not the 80% that just requires a fast inbox.
