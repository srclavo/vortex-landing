What Generic AI Employees Do Well

GoHighLevel's and Podium's tools work. They answer inbound calls, handle appointment requests, send reminders, and escalate to staff. For a practice with no automation at all, deploying either platform delivers immediate ROI.

What They Don't Know

Generic AI employees are trained on broad datasets from every industry. They're configured with scripts, not operational context.

They don't know that your practice runs 20-minute new patient intakes but 10-minute follow-ups. They don't recognize when a caller describing chest discomfort needs to be transferred to clinical staff — not scheduled. They don't know your EHR, your referral partners, or the specific insurance plans you accept.

They answer. They don't operate.

The Cost of a Generic Approach in a Medical Practice

The average medical practice misses 23% of inbound calls during business hours. At an average patient value of $300 per visit and 40 calls per day, that's roughly $2,700 in missed revenue weekly — before factoring in referral loss and no-show friction.

An AI employee that answers every call and books appointments is worth something. An AI employee that books the wrong appointment type because it doesn't know the difference between a new patient and a follow-up adds a different kind of cost: staff time, patient frustration, and scheduling chaos.

The ROI on AI employees depends almost entirely on how well they're configured for your specific operations.
